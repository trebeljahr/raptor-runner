Raptor Runner

A handcrafted endless runner through a desert that changes around you — the Chrome dinosaur game grown into a small, full-featured indie release.

Play now — free View on itch.io

Runs in your browser. No download, no account, no ads.

A desert that's actually alive

The sky cycles through twelve color bands, from warm dawn pastels to a starlit night with a Milky Way band and a moon on a realistic arc. Rain builds from scattered drops to a full storm with lightning — and everything on the ground is tinted by the sky, so dusk genuinely reads as dusk.

Small game, finished properly

  • 27 achievements — milestones, near-misses, storm survival
  • 5 rare easter-egg events: UFO, Santa, comet, tumbleweed, meteor
  • Coin chains — ten in a row earns a diamond
  • Pterodactyls at two altitudes: duck one, jump the other
  • Persistent personal bests and shareable score cards
  • Full controller support — Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo
  • Independent audio sliders for music, SFX, and rain
  • Installable as an offline PWA
  • No ads, no tracking cookies, no microtransactions
  • 1–10 minute runs — perfect coffee-break length

From weekend prototype to real release

Raptor Runner started in 2022 as a four-evening "what if the Chrome dino game had weather?" experiment, then sat in a folder for almost four years. In 2026 it was revived and finished — properly — as the first in a series of half-finished side projects being brought back to life with AI-assisted workflows. Read the full story, or follow the series at ricos.site.
